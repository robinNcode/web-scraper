## Abstract  
Salman Khan (b. 27 December 1965, Indore) is an Indian actor, film producer, and television personality prominent in Hindi cinema. Over a three‑decade career he has acted in more than 80 films, received two National Film Awards (as producer) and two Filmfare Awards (as actor), and repeatedly headlined the highest‑grossing Hindi releases of ten individual years. His filmography spans romance, action, comedy and drama, with notable milestones such as *Maine Pyar Kiya* (1989), *Hum Aapke Hain Koun..!* (1994), *Wanted* (2009), and *Dabangg* series (2010‑2012). Khan also hosts the reality show *Bigg Boss* and leads the charitable Being Human Foundation. His public image is marked by substantial commercial success, a large fan base, and recurring legal controversies, including convictions related to a vehicular homicide case (2015) and a black‑buck poaching case (2018).
